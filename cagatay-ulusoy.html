About Çağatay Ulusoy

Çağatay Ulusoy is a Turkish actor and model known for his work in television series and films. He has appeared in popular Turkish productions and has gained an international audience through television and streaming projects.

Career

Çağatay Ulusoy has worked across Turkish television, cinema and streaming productions. His roles in drama and action projects have helped him become a recognized name among Turkish and international audiences.

Popular Works

His notable television and streaming work includes Adını Feriha Koydum, Medcezir, İçerde and The Protector, along with other Turkish productions.
