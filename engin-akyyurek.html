Engin Akyürek

Turkish Actor, Writer & Screenwriter

About Engin Akyürek

Engin Akyürek is a Turkish actor known for his work in television series and films. He graduated from Ankara University and began his professional acting career after participating in the television competition Türkiye'nin Yıldızları.

Profile Information

Name Engin Akyürek
Born 12 October 1981
Birthplace Ankara, Turkey
Profession Actor, Writer & Screenwriter

Popular Works

His well-known television work includes Fatmagül'ün Suçu Ne?, Kara Para Aşk, Sefirin Kızı, Kaçış and other Turkish productions.

Career

Engin Akyürek started his television career with Yabancı Damat and later appeared in numerous television series and films. His performances have earned him recognition in Turkey and internationally.
